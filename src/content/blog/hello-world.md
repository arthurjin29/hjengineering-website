We're launching this blog to share what we learn from our engineering work across Australia's crane and construction industry.

## What to expect

We'll write about the engineering problems we encounter and the approaches we take to solve them — from crane lift planning and temporary works design to the computational tools we build along the way.

Topics will include:

- **Crane engineering** — lift planning, outrigger loads, ground bearing, and rigging design
- **Temporary works** — practical approaches to scaffold checks, falsework, and stability analysis
- **Engineering tools** — updates on our sling length calculator, access map, and other tools we're developing
- **Standards and practice** — how we apply AS 3775, AS 4100, and other Australian Standards in practice

## Why a blog?

Most engineering consultancies don't publish their working notes. We think that's a missed opportunity. The crane and construction industry benefits when engineers share practical knowledge — not just theory, but the real-world considerations that shape good engineering decisions.

We'll keep posts practical, concise, and grounded in the work we actually do.

Stay tuned.
